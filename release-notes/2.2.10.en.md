## Improved

- Drawing consecutive strokes no longer stutters while wobble playback is
  paused. Ugurugu no longer prepares unseen animation frames in the background
  after every stroke; it waits until playback resumes. The next stroke stays
  responsive even in large pictures with many layers and strokes or a moved
  selection.
- Finishing a stroke now reuses the result it just drew on screen instead of
  preparing the same frame once more in the background, removing the last
  bit of background work that used to follow every stroke while playback is
  paused.
- Pausing playback or starting a new stroke now interrupts in-progress
  background frame preparation right away. Previously a job that had already
  started kept rendering a whole frame before stopping, which could weigh on
  drawing responsiveness in the meantime.
