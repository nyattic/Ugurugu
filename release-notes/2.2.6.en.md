## New

- Tablet pressure can now be turned on or off from either the Brush or Eraser
  settings. The shared choice applies to both tools and is remembered after
  restarting the app.
- Selection transforms now offer **Smooth** and **Pixel-based** methods in the
  selection controls and menu. Smooth remains the default, while Pixel-based
  avoids blending neighboring pixel values. This prevents new semitransparent
  edge pixels from being introduced into opaque artwork while preserving
  transparency already present in the source, though edges may look stepped.
  The preview and applied transform use the same method.

## Improved

- Brush preset cards take less vertical space, so more presets remain visible
  at once.

## Fixed

- Brush presets and selection transform controls now fit inside narrow tool
  docks instead of being clipped along the side.
