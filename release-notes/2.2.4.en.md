## New

- You can now rotate the canvas freely without changing the artwork. Hold
  `Shift+Space` and drag with a mouse or pen, or use `Shift` with the scroll
  wheel.
- Rotate in 5° steps with `-` and `^`, or use **View → Rotate canvas** and the
  status bar to enter an exact angle or return to 0°. Zoom, pan, mirroring,
  pen position, and pressure all continue to work while the canvas is rotated.

## Fixed

- Copying a selection also placed strokes and images outside the selection on
  the clipboard. The visible result is unchanged, but unrelated source data is
  no longer included.
- Auto select and Paint bucket now work on layers inside groups and on layers
  clipped to the layer below.
- Strokes, text, and fills are no longer added invisibly to layers inside a
  hidden group. The app now explains why the operation cannot be completed.
- Recovery autosave failures are no longer silent. The status bar reports the
  problem while it continues, and a notification appears on the first failure.
