WagleWaglePaint leaves beta with 1.0. Thank you to everyone who reported
bugs along the way.

## Improved

- The memory the on-screen preview uses while you draw on a large canvas is
  now managed against a single budget. The preview caches could previously
  exceed the declared limit, so working zoomed in on a 4096×4096 canvas used
  noticeably more memory than expected.
- Because of that change, the on-screen preview is rendered at a slightly
  lower resolution when a canvas close to 4096×4096 is viewed at around 100%.
  Saved and exported images are unaffected.
