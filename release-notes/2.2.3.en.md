## New

- A **Text tool** (`T`) joins the left tool bar. Click the canvas to place
  the text, drag it into position, then press `Enter` to apply or `Esc`
  to cancel.
- The tool settings panel holds the content (multiple lines work), the
  font, the size, and a **Fill the letters** toggle.
- The letter outlines are drawn with your current brush color, width, and
  texture, and wobble like hand-drawn lines. Filled interiors hold still,
  just like paint bucket fills.
- Applied text becomes ordinary strokes: select, move, and resize it like
  anything else, and a single undo removes it whole. The file format is
  unchanged, so projects still save as the same `.ugu`. Applied text can
  no longer be edited by retyping.

## Fixed

- In the Korean and Japanese interfaces, the left tool bar grew a little
  wider the first time each tool was clicked. The buttons were picking up
  shortcut suffixes on their labels.
