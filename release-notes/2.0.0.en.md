Ugurugu 2.0.0 is the release where WagleWaglePaint becomes Ugurugu. The
name is not the only change: this update also widens the whole drawing
experience, from how lines move to coloring, opening older work, and
sharing the finished result.

## The app is now called Ugurugu

- The new name installs as a separate app, so the automatic update does not
  carry you across. Please download the installer from the Releases page.
  Your settings and shortcuts move over on the first launch.
- The previous app stays where it is, so you can remove it once you are
  comfortable with the new one.
- Newly saved projects use the `.ugu` extension.

## More ways to make lines move

- Alongside the familiar classic movement, you can choose smooth movement or
  a stepped, stop-motion-like style.
- Adjust how detailed the movement feels, how closely lines move together,
  and how much surprise is added.
- Add playful gaps to make a line look naturally broken.
- Let erased areas move with the rest of the drawing.
- Wobble controls that were spread around the timeline are now together in
  one easy-to-reach menu.

## Drawing and editing

- The paint bucket can now decide how similar nearby colors need to be. It can
  use the current layer, layers you mark as references, or everything visible
  as its guide.
- The area tool can paint as well as select. Draw a freehand, rectangular, or
  oval shape and fill it immediately.
- Place a photo or another drawing on a new layer, then move, resize, or rotate
  it. Repeated resizing always starts from the original image.
- Layers can be merged with the one below when doing so keeps the same look.

## Files and export

- Open `.wawa` files saved by WiggleWiggleTool 10 and continue working on
  them. They open as new projects without changing the originals.
- Export animations as looping WebP files. As with GIF, you can choose the size
  and whether to keep a transparent background.
- Save your favorite brush, eraser, fill, and movement setup as a `.wwpreset`
  file and load it again later.

## Easier to use

- Choose the highlight color used around the app.
- Press `F1` at any time to open the built-in help.
- The app now uses new icon artwork drawn by seuppi.

## For existing projects

- Older `.wagle` and `.wobble` files still open, and existing drawings keep
  their original appearance.
- Files saved by 2.0.0 can contain new features and may not open in an older
  version. Keep a copy of important work before updating.
- `.wawa` files are brought across as closely as the two apps allow. Some
  wobble, airbrush, and filled shapes may look different, and the app explains
  what changed after opening the file.
