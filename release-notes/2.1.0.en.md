2.1.0 removes the stalls that interrupted playback while you were drawing,
and gives the tools and colours a new home on screen.

## New

- Wobble can be switched off one layer at a time. The button beside the eye
  in the layer list holds that layer still, and pressing it again puts the
  layer back on the drawing's settings. Use it to keep a background
  perfectly still while the lines above it keep moving.
- To give a layer its own strength as well, switch the range at the top of
  the **Wobble** panel to **Active layer**.
- The layer list shows each layer's opacity and blend mode above its name,
  so you no longer have to click through layers to find the one you set to
  multiply.
- Tool options that used to be hidden behind a long press now live in a
  **Tool settings** panel that is always on screen. Fold away the parts you
  are not using, section by section. The tool list on the left is icons only
  now that the panel names the tool you picked.
- Picking a colour has been redone. Choose the colour on the ring, then set
  its brightness and strength with either a square or a triangle, whichever
  suits you.
- One button folds the whole panel away when you want more room. Nothing is
  left behind, and unfolding puts every panel, tab order, and size back
  exactly where it was. A folded panel stays folded after you quit and
  reopen the app.
- Narrowing a panel no longer cuts off what is inside it. The contents rearrange
  onto more rows instead.
- There is now an eyedropper. Press `I` or pick it from the list on the left
  to take a colour straight from your drawing.
- Fill a selected area with the brush colour in one step (`Alt+Delete`).
- Fold the animation bar at the bottom away and back (`Ctrl+T`).

## Improved

- Playback is far smoother. It used to stop or stutter in all of these
  situations:
  - It stayed frozen the whole time your pen was down.
  - It paused for seconds after you changed a wobble setting.
  - It started over from scratch every time you played or paused.
  - It stuttered for a long while when you drew with playback paused and
    then pressed play.
- On computers with plenty of memory, more of the animation is kept ready in
  advance, so playback runs more smoothly.
- On Windows, the update window now shows what is new right away.

## Fixed

- Using undo or redo in the middle of a stroke let that unfinished stroke
  land on the drawing when you lifted the pen, and the step you had just
  undone could no longer be redone. The stroke in progress is now dropped,
  and so is a selection you were drawing with the area select tool.
- Merging a layer down could let its erased areas cut into the artwork
  underneath. Merging is now refused in that case, with an explanation.
- The brush list below the pen, marker, airbrush and spray tabs shifted
  slightly every time you switched between them.

## Changed

- Files saved by 2.1.0 can carry per-layer movement settings and may not open
  in an older version. Keep a copy of important work before updating.
- Files made by older versions still open, and your drawings keep their
  original appearance.
