## Fixed

- A layer holding only an inserted image, with nothing drawn on it, could
  disappear when the canvas or the image was resized. Saving afterwards made
  the loss permanent.
- Layers with an inserted image, and layers merged with the one below, could
  be treated as empty even when artwork was inside the selection, which
  disabled moving and deleting.
- Copying a selection that contains an image pasted without the image, or did
  not paste at all. Strokes with non-default wobble settings also looked
  different from the screen when pasted into another app.
- Undoing after a selection was moved or transformed restored the artwork but
  left the selection outline at its previous position, so the next move or
  delete changed a different place than the one shown.
- Wide strokes drawn with a square-tipped brush, such as the markers, could
  have their ends or corners clipped while drawing.
- Export underestimated the memory it needed, so it could fail at a size the
  dialog had presented as safe. This happened with documents whose layers were
  merged repeatedly, or whose canvas was resized partway through the work.
- Drawing or exporting a picture with many filled areas kept building up
  memory.
- Opening a `.wawa` file used far more memory than necessary.
