## Improved

- Drawing no longer slows down as a stroke grows, including when the pen is
  shaken quickly. Input stays as responsive at the end of a long stroke as it
  was at its start.

## Fixed

- Size and width fields no longer ignore what you type after clicking the
  `px` or `%` part to the right of the number. The caret now lands at the end
  of the number instead.
- Fixed a semitransparent stroke that crosses itself showing one color while
  you draw it and another once it is committed.
