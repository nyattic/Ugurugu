## Improved

- Drawing no longer drags as a picture fills up. Finishing a stroke used to
  redraw every frame of the wobble from scratch; now only the area around the
  new stroke is redrawn. Pictures with many layers and strokes, with groups or
  clipping, or where a selection has been moved, gain the most.
- A rotated canvas no longer slows the whole window down. Turning the canvas
  from the status bar, and playing the wobble while it is turned, are both
  noticeably quicker.

## Fixed

- The pen pressure setting in the brush and eraser panels showed a bare
  checkbox with no name beside it. It reads **PEN PRESSURE** again.
- Adding a layer after making a group no longer skips a number. Layer 1,
  Layer 2 and Group 1 are now followed by Layer 3 rather than Layer 4.
