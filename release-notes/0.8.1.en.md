## New

- Added `Hard`, `Soft`, and `Kneaded` eraser presets. Their cards preview the
  actual erasing texture, and each preset remembers its size and stabilization
  strength. The last selected preset is also restored after restarting the app.
- The Magic Wand can now reference the `Active layer`, marked `Reference
  layers`, or `All visible layers`. Paint layers can be marked as references in
  the Layers panel, and the setting is saved with the project.
- Added Rectangle and Ellipse modes alongside Freehand selection. All three
  modes work with both mouse and tablet input.

## Improved

- Replaced the global stroke stabilization setting with independent settings
  for every brush and eraser preset. Strength can be adjusted directly in each
  tool popover, and the previous global value is migrated automatically.
- Redesigned Magic Wand reference and selection-shape options as visual cards
  consistent with the brush preset interface.

## Fixed

- Undoing a move of selected strokes now restores their position without also
  clearing the selection.
- Pressing `Esc` after confirming a selection rotation now deselects while
  keeping the applied rotation.
