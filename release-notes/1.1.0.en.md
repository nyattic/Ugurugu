## New

- The selection can now be copied (Ctrl+C), cut (Ctrl+X), and pasted
  (Ctrl+V). Pasting always creates a new layer and never touches the
  active layer. The Copy and paste button on the selection action bar
  copies onto a new layer in a single click.
- Content pasted inside WagleWaglePaint arrives as live strokes rather
  than frozen pixels, so it keeps wobbling and stays editable. Pasting
  into other apps hands over a regular image. Pasting images copied from
  other apps is not supported yet.
- Added Select all (Ctrl+A) and Invert selection (Ctrl+Shift+I).
- In the area select and auto select tools, Shift+drag adds to the
  selection and Alt+drag subtracts from it. The Alt eyedropper for the
  brush, eraser, and paint bucket is unchanged.

## Changed

- Ctrl+D now deselects, matching the convention of other drawing tools.
  Duplicate selection remains available in the menu and the action bar,
  and you can assign it a new key in the Shortcuts tab of the settings.
- Existing artwork is unaffected. Saved .wagle documents render pixel
  for pixel the same as in previous versions.
