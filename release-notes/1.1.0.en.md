## New

- The selection can now be copied (Ctrl+C), cut (Ctrl+X), and pasted
  (Ctrl+V). Copying places a slightly offset copy on a new layer and keeps
  it selected, so you can drag it right away. The original layer stays
  unchanged. Paste adds what you copied to another document or window as
  a new layer.
- Lines copied and pasted inside WagleWaglePaint keep wobbling and can
  still be edited. Pasting into another app creates a regular image.
  Images copied from other apps cannot be pasted into WagleWaglePaint yet.
- Added Select all (Ctrl+A) and Invert selection (Ctrl+Shift+I).
- In the area select and auto select tools, Shift+drag adds to the
  selection and Alt+drag subtracts from it. The Alt eyedropper for the
  brush, eraser, and paint bucket is unchanged.

## Improved

- Large animated drawings now respond more smoothly when you draw or erase
  long lines. The app pauses less when you lift the pen, and the animation
  continues more smoothly after editing.

## Fixed

- Windows now shows an error message when an update cannot be downloaded.

## Changed

- Ctrl+D now deselects, matching the convention of other drawing tools.
- Duplicate selection, which stacked the copy on the same layer, has
  been replaced by copying. Use Copy (Ctrl+C) to duplicate content.
- Roughness controls have been removed from the brush settings and line
  properties. Lines already in your drawings keep the same appearance.
- Existing drawings are unaffected and look the same when opened in this
  version.
