## New

- Added adjustable stroke stabilization. Choose a strength from 0–100% in
  the drawing settings to smooth pointer and tablet input before a stroke is
  recorded.
- Layers now support Multiply, Screen, and Overlay blend modes in addition to
  Normal.
- Added nested layer groups with group-level visibility, opacity, blend modes,
  reordering, and thumbnails.
- Added “Clip to layer below” for constraining a layer to the alpha of its base
  layer within the same group.
- Selected strokes can now be edited non-destructively. Change paint and fill
  colors, or adjust paint and eraser width and roughness from the selection
  action bar or menu.

## Improved

- Layer hierarchy, clipping, blend modes, and stroke property edits all
  participate in undo and redo.
- The project format now preserves groups and clipping while continuing to
  open projects created by earlier versions.
